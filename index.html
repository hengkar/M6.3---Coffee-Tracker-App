Getting Started

Coffee Tracker helps you monitor your daily coffee consumption. Add each coffee you drink with details like type, size, cost, and rating.

Features

  • Track: Log every cup with detailed information
  • Search & Filter: Find specific entries using advanced filters
  • Export/Import: Backup your data in CSV or JSON format
  • Achievements: Unlock badges as you track more coffee
  • Dark Mode: Switch between light and dark themes

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Enter - Add coffee entry
  • Tab - Navigate between fields
  • Esc - Close modals
